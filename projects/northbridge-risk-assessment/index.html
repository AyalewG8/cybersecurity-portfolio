Project 01 · Risk Assessment

Northbridge Logistics Risk Assessment

A risk-based defense strategy for a fictional 150-employee logistics company across three offices, 180 endpoints, and 20 servers.

Objective

Identify the most important business risks and convert them into a practical, prioritized security roadmap using NIST SP 800-30 concepts, CVSS prioritization, and MITRE ATT&CK awareness.
