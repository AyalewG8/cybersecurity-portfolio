Project 01 · Risk Management

Northbridge Logistics Risk Assessment

Cybersecurity risk assessment and defense strategy for a fictional 150-employee logistics company operating across three offices with 180 endpoints and 20 servers.

Objective

Identify business risks, prioritize threats, and build a practical 90-day security roadmap using NIST SP 800-30 concepts, CVSS prioritization, and MITRE ATT&CK awareness.

Critical / High Risks

Phishing & spear phishing · Ransomware · Insider threat · MITM / Wi-Fi exposure · Credential attacks

My Role

Assessed assets, threats, vulnerabilities, and business impact; mapped controls; documented ownership and implementation sequencing.

Defense-in-Depth

NGFWMFAEDRPatch ManagementEmail SecurityWPA3VLAN SegmentationSIEM/SOARBackups/DR

90-Day Roadmap

0–30 days → MFA enforcement 30–60 days → EDR deployment + patch management 60–90 days → WAF + penetration testing + awareness training

Key Learning

Security is not a single product. Effective defense requires technology, processes, people, ownership, and continuous improvement working together.
