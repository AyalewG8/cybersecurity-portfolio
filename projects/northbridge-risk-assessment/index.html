Northbridge Logistics Risk Assessment

Cybersecurity risk assessment and defense strategy for a fictional 150-employee logistics company operating across three offices with 180 endpoints and 20 servers.

Identify business risks, prioritize threats, and build a practical 90-day security roadmap using NIST SP 800-30 concepts, CVSS prioritization, and MITRE ATT&CK awareness.
