Project 02 · Cryptography & Network Security

SecureHealth Cryptography & Network Security

A protected-health-information architecture securing EHR systems, cloud services, and partner connections with strong encryption, PKI, and disciplined key management.

Objective

Protect PHI in transit and at rest while reducing exposure to eavesdropping, man-in-the-middle attacks, ransomware, insider threats, and service disruption in a HIPAA/HITECH-aware design.

My Role

Designed the layered trust model, selected cryptographic controls, defined secure network zones, and documented certificate and key-lifecycle requirements.

Threats Addressed

Eavesdropping · MITM · Ransomware · Insider threats · Weak key handling · Denial-of-service

Outcome & Key Learning

The design shows that strong cryptography depends on more than algorithm selection. Secure generation, storage, rotation, validation, revocation, and monitoring of keys and certificates are what make protection sustainable.
