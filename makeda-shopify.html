Security Awareness

Trust matters in every online store.

This project also demonstrates awareness of the basic security practices that help protect customers and store administrators.

Admin protection

Use a unique password and multi-factor authentication for Shopify administrator accounts.

Least privilege

Give store staff only the permissions required for their role.

Secure checkout

Use Shopify’s secure checkout capabilities rather than storing payment information independently.

Customer privacy

Handle customer data carefully and communicate privacy practices clearly.
